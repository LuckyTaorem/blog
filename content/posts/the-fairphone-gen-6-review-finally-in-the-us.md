## Why the Gen 6+ Matters

The Fairphone Gen 6+ is more than a new smartphone; it is a statement that ethical manufacturing can coexist with flagship performance. Launching at $650, it sits comfortably between the premium offerings of Apple and Samsung while offering a 12‑GB RAM, 256‑GB internal storage, and a 50‑MP main camera that rivals many flagship devices. The device’s core value proposition is its longevity—seven years of software updates, a five‑year warranty, and a 10/10 iFixit repairability score. For consumers increasingly concerned about the environmental and social impact of electronics, the Gen 6+ offers a viable alternative that does not sacrifice power.

## Hardware & Performance Deep Dive

Under the hood, the Gen 6+ packs a Qualcomm Snapdragon 7s Gen 4, a mid‑tier processor that delivers solid performance for everyday tasks and moderate gaming. The 6.3‑inch 120‑Hz AMOLED display provides vibrant colors and smooth scrolling, while the 4,415‑mAh battery promises a “daylong” runtime, leaving roughly 20 % charge at bedtime after 5–6 hours of screen time. Key hardware highlights include:

- **RAM & Storage**: 12 GB of LPDDR5 RAM paired with 256 GB of UFS 3.1 storage ensures multitasking remains snappy.
- **Camera System**: A 50‑MP primary sensor, 13‑MP ultrawide, and 32‑MP selfie camera cover a wide range of photography needs.
- **Fingerprint Sensor**: Integrated into the side power button for quick access without a dedicated sensor.
- **Connectivity**: 5G support (non‑mmWave), Wi‑Fi 6E, and dual‑SIM eSIM capabilities (though eSIM on T‑Mobile is absent).
- **Durability**: IP55 rating provides resistance to dust and light rain, but it is not pool‑proof.

The device’s design also emphasizes modularity. The backplate can be swapped with accessories such as wallets or grips using just two screws, allowing users to customize the phone without opening the main chassis.

## Software & Sustainability Features

Fairphone’s commitment to sustainability extends beyond hardware. The phone ships with Android 16 and includes a proprietary “Fairphone Moments” mode, activated by a physical yellow switch on the right edge. This distraction‑free mode limits access to up to five selected apps, encouraging mindful usage. The emphasis on long‑term software support—seven years of updates through 2033—means the Gen 6+ will remain secure and functional well beyond the typical 2–3 year cycle of mainstream flagships.

The device also highlights the Fair Cobalt Alliance, a nonprofit co‑founded by Fairphone to support workers in cobalt mines. By featuring a cobalt‑blue color and a yellow‑accented button, the phone pays tribute to the miners whose labor powers many electronics. The brand’s transparency extends to parts availability: all replaceable components, including the display (replacement cost $90), will be sold through 2033, ensuring that users can repair or upgrade without obsolescence.

## Carrier & Connectivity Landscape

The Gen 6+ is available on several major carriers: T‑Mobile, AT&T, Google Fi, Boost Mobile, and Cricket. It is **not** supported on Verizon or Verizon‑based MVNOs, and it lacks millimeter‑wave 5G. While the device does not include wireless charging or a USB‑C cable in the box, its wired charging performance remains robust thanks to the 5‑A USB‑C port.

### Pricing & Value Proposition

At **$650**, the Gen 6+ sits in a sweet spot between the mid‑range and premium tiers. Compared with the iPhone 16 Pro (starting around $999) and the Samsung Galaxy S26 FE (around $749), the Fairphone offers:

- **More RAM** (12 GB vs. 8 GB on many competitors)
- **Higher base storage** (256 GB vs. 128 GB)
- **Longer software support** (7 years vs. 3–4 years)
- **Full repairability** (10/10 iFixit score)

For buyers who prioritize sustainability, the extra cost over a typical mid‑range phone is justified by the extended lifespan and ethical sourcing narrative. For those whose primary concern is raw performance or carrier breadth, the trade‑offs may feel more pronounced.

### Final Verdict

Fairphone’s Gen 6+ proves that a smartphone can be both **responsibly made** and **competitively spec’d**. It isn’t a pure flagship—its processor sits a notch below the absolute top‑end, and the omission of some convenience features may turn off power users. However, the combination of a 12‑GB RAM, 50‑MP main camera, 120‑Hz AMOLED display, and a **seven‑year software roadmap** creates a compelling package for anyone willing to look beyond the usual brand loyalty.

If you value a phone that you can **repair yourself**, that **supports ethical mining practices**, and that **won’t become obsolete in three years**, the Fairphone Gen 6+ is arguably the most sensible flagship‑class purchase on the market today.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will the Fairphone Gen 6+ receive Android updates beyond 2033?**  
A: The company has committed to seven years of major OS updates and security patches, ending in 2033. After that, community‑driven ROMs are expected to keep the device usable.

**Q: Can I use the phone with a Verizon SIM if I switch carriers later?**  
A: No. The hardware does not support Verizon’s network bands, so the device will not connect to Verizon’s LTE or 5G services.

**Q: Does the phone support dual‑SIM operation?**  
A: Yes. It features a physical nano‑SIM slot plus eSIM support, though eSIM activation on T‑Mobile is currently unavailable.

**Q: What accessories are available for the modular backplate?**  
A: Fairphone sells a range of snap‑on accessories, including a leather wallet, a carbon‑fiber grip, and a magnetic car mount. All attach with two Torx‑T3 screws.

**Q: Is a charger included in the box?**  
A: No. The box contains the phone, a USB‑C to USB‑C cable, and a SIM ejector tool. Users must purchase a charger separately.

**Q: How does the “Fairphone Moments” mode differ from Android’s built‑in Digital Wellbeing?**  
A: Moments uses a hardware switch to instantly lock the device into a predefined app whitelist, providing a tactile, offline method to enforce focus, whereas Digital Wellbeing relies on software timers and notifications.

**Q: Are repair guides available for the Gen 6+?**  
A: Yes. Fairphone publishes detailed step‑by‑step repair manuals and video guides on its website, and the iFixit community has already contributed additional tips.

**Q: What is the expected battery lifespan?**  
A: The 4,415 mAh battery is rated for 500 full charge cycles before capacity drops to about 80 % of its original capacity. Because the battery is user‑replaceable, you can swap it out at any time.

**Q: How does the phone’s environmental impact compare to a typical flagship?**  
A: Fairphone reports a **30 % lower carbon footprint** than comparable devices, thanks to recycled materials, modular design, and responsible sourcing of conflict‑free minerals.
