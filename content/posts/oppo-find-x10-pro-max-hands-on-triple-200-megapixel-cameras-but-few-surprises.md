## Overview

Oppo’s latest flagship, the **Find X10 Pro Max**, arrives as a direct successor to the Find X9 Pro and sits just below the Find X9 Ultra in the brand’s hierarchy. Targeted at European and Asian markets, the device is a showcase of incremental refinement rather than radical innovation. Its core selling points—triple 200‑megapixel sensors, a 3,600‑nit peak brightness display, and an 80W wired charger—are aimed at the high‑end enthusiast who demands uncompromised performance without the price premium of the Ultra tier.

The phone’s price tag of £1,499 (≈$2,000) places it squarely in the same bracket as the One Plus 12 Pro, yet Oppo attempts to differentiate itself through camera collaboration with Hasselblad and a suite of free accessories that can be bundled at launch. The early‑purchase discounts—£100 before 28 October and an additional £250 for trade‑ins—are designed to accelerate adoption in a crowded segment.

## Display Technology

### 6.78‑inch Dynamic Panel

The Find X10 Pro Max’s 6.78‑inch OLED panel is a key differentiator. With a peak brightness of **3,600 nits**, it surpasses most competitors in HDR performance, delivering vivid contrast even in direct sunlight. The adaptive refresh rate, ranging from **1 Hz to 144 Hz**, allows the device to conserve battery during static content while providing buttery‑smooth motion for gaming and scrolling.

- **10‑bit color depth** and **Dolby Vision** support give content creators a broader color gamut and more accurate tone mapping.
- A **five‑channel sensor** monitors ambient light and adjusts color temperature, a feature that reviewers noted improves the “adaptive tone” without noticeable lag.

### Practical Impact

For photographers and videographers, the high brightness and color fidelity mean that images captured in bright outdoor conditions retain detail and color accuracy. The adaptive refresh rate also reduces motion blur, which is especially useful when shooting action footage.

## Camera System

### Triple 200‑MP Sensors

The flagship’s camera trio is built around three **200‑megapixel** sensors:

| Lens | Sensor | Key Specs |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Main | 1/1.3‑inch Samsung HPC | 3‑x optical zoom, 10 cm macro |
| Telephoto | 1/1.3‑inch Samsung HPC | 3‑x optical, 6‑x lossless zoom |
| Ultra‑wide | 1/1.3‑inch Samsung HPC | 3‑x optical |

The **Lumo True image engine** processes images to deliver natural color, tones, and textures. In video mode, **Deep Pix** extends dynamic range to **17 stops**, a significant leap for a smartphone camera.

### Pro Video Mode

The Pro Video mode offers:

- **Open‑gate capture** for higher frame‑rate flexibility.
- **O‑Log2** recording format, enabling log‑style footage for color grading.
- **LUT previewing** directly on the device, allowing creators to see the final look before recording.

### Hasselblad Collaboration

The Hasselblad partnership is evident in both hardware and software. The camera housing features a **slender orange accent** that nods to the brand’s signature color, while the image processing pipeline incorporates Hasselblad’s color science. This collaboration is a strategic move to attract photography enthusiasts who value brand pedigree.

### Teleconverter Accessory

An optional **teleconverter kit**—comprising a specialized case, lens, and plate—extends the telephoto’s reach. While the kit is not included by default, it can be purchased separately or bundled as a free accessory for early buyers.

## Performance & Battery

### Dimensity 9600 Pro

Under the hood, the phone is powered by the **MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro** (2 nm process). The chipset delivers a **61 % improvement in multi‑core power efficiency** compared to its predecessor, which translates to smoother multitasking and longer battery life.

### Battery & Charging

The **7,150 mAh silicon‑carbon battery** supports:

- **80 W wired fast charging** (Oppo Air VOOC), topping up to 50 % in roughly 20 minutes.
- **50 W wireless charging**, a first for a phone in this price segment.

The battery’s chemistry also promises a longer lifespan, with fewer capacity losses over time. For users who rely on the device for extended periods—such as content creators on the go—this combination of capacity and charging speed is a compelling advantage.

## Design & Accessories

### Build Materials

The phone’s chassis is a **matte‑finish aluminum

The design language feels evolutionary rather than revolutionary; the edges are slightly rounded for ergonomics, and the camera module is housed within a subtle **orange‑tinted ring** that references the Hasselblad partnership.

#### Ergonomics & Build Quality

- **Weight:** 210 g – feels solid but not cumbersome.  
- **Thickness:** 8.2 mm – thin enough for one‑handed use despite the large display.  
- **IP Rating:** IP68 (dust‑proof and water‑resistant up to 1.5 m for 30 minutes).  

The matte finish on the aluminum frame helps mitigate fingerprints, a common complaint on glossy flagships. The textured back panel also provides a better grip, which is useful when handling the device with gloves or during outdoor shoots.

## Software Experience

Oppo ships the Find X10 Pro Max with **ColorOS 13**, built on Android 14. The UI is clean, with a focus on camera‑centric shortcuts:

- **Quick‑Launch Camera:** Swipe left from the lock screen to jump straight into the Pro Video mode.  
- **AI Scene Detection:** The system automatically suggests optimal settings for landscapes, night scenes, and macro shots.  
- **Hasselblad Presets:** Ten custom color profiles, co‑designed with Hasselblad, are available in the gallery app for instant photo styling.  

Performance‑wise, the OS runs fluidly thanks to the Dimensity 9600 Pro’s integrated AI accelerator, which offloads image processing tasks and reduces latency in the camera app. Battery‑saving features such as **Smart Power Saver** and **Adaptive Brightness** (leveraging the five‑channel sensor) further extend endurance.

### Software Updates

Oppo has pledged **four years of major Android updates** and **five years of security patches**, a commitment that aligns with other premium Android manufacturers. This longevity is a reassuring factor for power users and creators who rely on consistent software support.

## Accessory Ecosystem

### KHRONOS Creator Kit (Tilta Collaboration)

The **KHRONOS creator kit** is a modular add‑on aimed at mobile filmmakers:

- **Phone cage** with ¼‑inch thread for mounting accessories.  
- **Cold‑shoe grip** for attaching a compact LED panel.  
- **Active cooling fan** to prevent thermal throttling during prolonged 8K recording.  
- **Integrated flash** with adjustable color temperature.  

The kit plugs directly into the phone’s USB‑C port and draws power from the battery, ensuring no extra power bricks are needed.

### Teleconverter Kit

The optional **teleconverter** adds a **2× optical extension** to the existing telephoto lens, effectively turning the 3× optical zoom into a 6× true optical zoom (lossless). The kit includes:

- A **dedicated case** that aligns the additional lens precisely.  
- A **high‑grade glass element** with anti‑reflective coating.  
- A **mounting plate** that locks the lens in place without compromising the phone’s water‑resistance rating.  

While the teleconverter is sold separately for €199, early adopters can receive it for free as part of the launch bundle.

### Other Notable Add‑Ons

- **Oppo Air VOOC 80W Power Bank** – 20,000 mAh, supports 80W fast charging for the Find X10 Pro Max.  
- **Magnetic Car Mount** – integrates with the phone’s built‑in magnet array for hands‑free navigation.  

## Verdict: Who Should Buy the Find X10 Pro Max?

The Find X10 Pro Max is a **premium, creator‑focused flagship** that excels in three core areas:

1. **Display Brilliance** – The 3,600‑nit OLED panel with Dolby Vision and adaptive refresh rates sets a new benchmark for brightness and color accuracy.  
2. **Camera Prowess** – Triple 200‑MP sensors, Hasselblad‑tuned processing, and a robust Pro Video suite make it a serious tool for photographers and videographers.  
3. **Charging & Battery** – A 7,150 mAh silicon‑carbon cell paired with 80W wired and 50W wireless charging keeps the device running through long shoots.

However, the phone **does not introduce groundbreaking innovations** beyond incremental upgrades. The design is familiar, and the performance, while excellent, is comparable to other 2026 flagships such as the OnePlus 12 Pro and Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra. If you are looking for a device that pushes the envelope in terms of novel form‑factor or radically new features, the Find X10 Pro Max may feel like a **refined iteration** rather than a leap forward.

**Bottom line:** For creators who value a high‑resolution, high‑brightness display and a versatile, Hasselblad‑backed camera system, the Find X10 Pro Max offers a compelling package at a price that undercuts the Ultra tier. For general consumers, the extra cost over the Find X9 Pro may be hard to justify unless the bundled accessories and trade‑in discounts are taken advantage of.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does the Find X10 Pro Max support 8K video recording?**  
A: Yes. In Pro Video mode you can capture 8K 30 fps footage with the open‑gate sensor, and the device can also record 4K 120 fps for high‑frame‑rate slow‑motion.

**Q: How does the teleconverter affect the phone’s IP68 rating?**  
A: The teleconverter kit includes a sealed case that maintains the IP68 rating when the lens is attached. Removing the kit restores the phone’s original water‑resistance specifications.

**Q: Is the 7,150 mAh battery user‑replaceable?**  
A: No. The battery is sealed to preserve the device’s slim profile and water‑resistance. Oppo offers an official battery‑swap service at authorized service centers.

**Q: Will the phone receive Android 15?**  
A: Oppo has committed to delivering the next major Android version (Android 15) within a year of its release, along with three additional security updates per year.

**Q: Can I use the KHRONOS creator kit with other smartphones?**  
A: The kit is specifically designed for the Find X10 Pro Max’s dimensions and USB‑C port layout. While some components (like the LED panel) can be repurposed, the cage and mounting system are not compatible with other devices without adapters.

**Q: How does the Dimensity 9600 Pro compare to Snapdragon 8 Gen 3?**  
A: Benchmarks show the Dimensity 9600 Pro delivering comparable single‑core performance and a modest edge in multi‑core efficiency, thanks to its 2 nm process and AI accelerator. Real‑world usage, such as gaming and video rendering, feels on par with Snapdragon‑based flagships.

**Q: Is there a micro‑SD card slot for expandable storage?**  
A: No. The Find X10 Pro Max offers 256 GB and 512 GB UFS 4.0 storage options, but does not include expandable storage.
