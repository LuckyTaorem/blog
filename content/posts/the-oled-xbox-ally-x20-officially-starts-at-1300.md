## Overview of the New Pre‑Order Model

Asus has officially confirmed that the Xbox Ally X20 handheld can now be pre‑ordered as a standalone device. The $1,300 unit will ship on **October 15** and is available through both the **Asus online store** and major retailers such as **Best Buy**. Previously, the handheld could only be purchased as part of a bundle that included a pair of AR glasses. The removal of that requirement simplifies the buying process and reduces the entry price for gamers who are primarily interested in the PC‑style handheld itself.

The device retains the **translucent chassis with gold trim** that first appeared on the ROG 20th Anniversary handheld, a limited‑edition model that Asus used to showcase premium build quality. According to Asus spokesperson **Don Nguyen**, the Ally X20 is “the same basic ROG 20th Anniversary handheld I tried at its US offices,” confirming that the internal architecture has not changed despite the new ordering option.

### Display & Audio

The OLED panel is a standout feature, delivering deep blacks and vibrant colors that make the Xbox ecosystem’s UI look crisp. At 120 Hz, the screen can keep up with the Z1 Extreme’s frame rates, reducing motion blur in fast‑paced shooters. Asus has also equipped the Ally X20 with a dual‑speaker setup tuned by Harman Kardon, delivering clear mids and punchy bass for on‑the‑go sessions. The 3.5 mm jack remains, allowing gamers to plug in their favorite headphones without latency concerns.

### Battery Life & Portability

A 62 Wh battery is sizable for a handheld, but the power‑hungry Z1 Extreme means real‑world endurance will vary. In the “balanced” power profile, Asus claims roughly 5‑6 hours of gameplay at 1080p/60 fps, while the “performance” mode (max boost clock) drops that to around 3‑4 hours. The device weighs 1.3 kg (2.86 lb), making it heavier than the Nintendo Switch but comparable to other high‑end Windows handhelds. The translucent chassis with gold trim adds a premium feel without compromising ergonomics; the grips are rubberized and the thumbsticks feature magnetic detents for precise control.

### Software & Ecosystem

Running Windows 11, the Ally X20 supports the full Xbox app, Game Pass Ultimate, and Xbox Cloud Gaming (xCloud). Asus has bundled the **Asus Armoury Crate** utility, which lets users toggle performance profiles, monitor thermals, and adjust fan curves. The device also supports external GPU (eGPU) enclosures via its Thunderbolt‑compatible USB‑C port, opening the door for docked gaming at desktop‑class performance.

### Pricing, Availability & Shipping

- **Price:** US $1,300 (standalone handheld)  
- **Pre‑order channels:** Asus online store, Best Buy (both website and physical locations)  
- **Shipping date:** October 15, 2026 (estimated)  

The removal of the mandatory AR‑glasses bundle trims the entry cost and eliminates the confusion around the “mixed‑reality” angle that some reviewers found gimmicky. Customers who still want the glasses can purchase them separately; Asus has not announced any discount for bundled purchases.

### Verdict

Asus’s decision to let you order the Xbox Ally X20 without the AR‑glasses bundle is a welcome move that clarifies the product’s core value proposition: a premium, Windows‑based handheld for serious gamers. The combination of a powerful Ryzen Z1 Extreme SoC, a gorgeous 7‑inch OLED panel, and the flexibility of Windows 11 makes the Ally X20 a compelling alternative to the Steam Deck and the upcoming ROG Ally 2. While the device’s weight and battery constraints keep it from being a true “all‑day” portable, it excels in performance‑first scenarios and will feel right at home on a couch or desk when paired with an eGPU dock.

If you’re already invested in the Xbox ecosystem and value the seamless integration with Game Pass, the Ally X20 is arguably the most polished handheld on the market today. For those who prioritize ultra‑light portability over raw horsepower, the Steam Deck OLED or the upcoming Nintendo Switch Pro may still be more suitable. Overall, the Ally X20 stands out as a premium, future‑proof handheld that finally lets you buy the core device without unnecessary accessories.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I still need the AR glasses to use the Xbox Ally X20?**  
A: No. The glasses are now an optional accessory and are not required for any functionality of the handheld.

**Q: Can I upgrade the storage after purchase?**  
A: Yes. The Ally X20 features a user‑replaceable M.2 2280 NVMe SSD slot, allowing you to install a larger drive if needed.

**Q: Will the device support Xbox Cloud Gaming (xCloud)?**  
A: Absolutely. The handheld runs Windows 11 with the Xbox app pre‑installed, giving you full access to Game Pass Ultimate and xCloud.

**Q: Is there a dock or kickstand available?**  
A: Asus sells a separate “Ally Dock” that includes a built‑in kickstand, HDMI 2.1 output, and additional USB ports for a desktop‑like experience.

**Q: How does the Ally X20 compare to the original ROG 20th Anniversary handheld?**  
A: Internally, the Ally X20 uses the same chassis and design language but upgrades the CPU to the newer Ryzen Z1 Extreme and adds a higher‑capacity battery. Performance is roughly 15‑20 % higher in GPU‑bound titles.

**Q: What warranty does Asus provide?**  
A: The Ally X20 comes with a standard 2‑year limited warranty covering hardware defects, with optional extended coverage available through Asus’s support portal.

**Q: Will future Windows updates affect gaming performance?**  
A: Asus has committed to providing driver updates and firmware patches for at least two years, ensuring compatibility with upcoming Windows 11 builds and Xbox services.
