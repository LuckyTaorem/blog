## Technical Breakdown of the Yoga Slim 7X

The Snapdragon X2 Elite is Qualcomm’s flagship ARM chip for laptops. Its 12‑core design (four performance cores, four efficiency cores, and four AI‑optimized cores) delivers a balanced mix of raw compute and low‑power operation. The integrated **Adreno GPU** handles light gaming and media playback without the thermal throttling typical of many thin‑and‑light x86 CPUs.

### Display and Form Factor

- **Size & Resolution**: 14‑inch, 2560 × 1600 (2K) OLED, 16:10 aspect ratio.
- **Touch Capability**: Multi‑touch with low latency, ideal for creative work and note‑taking.

- **RAM**: 16 GB DDR5, offering ample headroom for multitasking and demanding Windows applications.
