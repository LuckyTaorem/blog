## Bluesky’s New Privacy Feature: Hiding Reposts from Specific Users

Bluesky, the decentralized social media platform, has rolled out a new privacy control that allows users to hide reposts from specific individuals in their feeds. This feature addresses a common frustration among social media users: the inundation of spammy or repetitive reposts from certain accounts. By giving users more granular control over their feed, Bluesky is reinforcing its commitment to user-centric design and customization.

The feature is simple to use. Users can click the three-dot menu on any profile and select **"Hide reposts in feeds."** Once enabled, reposts from that account will no longer appear in the user’s timeline, reducing clutter and improving the overall browsing experience. This move aligns with Bluesky’s broader mission to empower users with tools that enhance their control over content consumption.

For a platform that has positioned itself as an alternative to mainstream social media, this update is a strategic step toward differentiating itself from competitors like X (formerly Twitter). While X has focused on algorithmic curation and monetization, Bluesky is doubling down on user autonomy, a value proposition that resonates with privacy-conscious users.

## Technical Breakdown: How the Feature Works

Bluesky’s new privacy control is implemented at the user level, meaning it does not require changes to the underlying protocol or infrastructure. Here’s a step-by-step breakdown of how it works:

1. **Accessing the Feature**: Users navigate to the profile of the account whose reposts they want to hide.
2. **Enabling the Control**: Click the three-dot menu in the top-right corner of the profile and select **"Hide reposts in feeds."**
3. **Immediate Effect**: Once enabled, reposts from that account will no longer appear in the user’s feed, though original posts from the account will still be visible.
4. **Reversibility**: Users can re-enable reposts at any time by revisiting the profile and deselecting the option.

### Auto-Numbering Posts in Threads (Beta)
In addition to the repost-hiding feature, Bluesky has also introduced a beta feature that auto-numbers posts in threads. This feature is designed to help users gauge the length of a thread at a glance, making it easier to navigate long conversations. Users can enable this feature in Bluesky’s settings, as announced by the platform to *The Verge*.

While the auto-numbering feature is still in beta, it reflects Bluesky’s iterative approach to development. By testing new features with a subset of users, the platform can gather feedback and refine the experience before a wider rollout.

## FAQ: Bluesky’s New Privacy Features

### **1. How do I hide reposts from a specific user on Bluesky?**
To hide reposts from a specific user, navigate to their profile, click the three-dot menu, and select **"Hide reposts in feeds."** This will prevent their reposts from appearing in your feed while still allowing their original posts to show up.

### **2. Can I still see original posts from a user whose reposts I’ve hidden?**
Yes. Hiding reposts from a user only affects their reposted content. Their original posts will still appear in your feed unless you mute or block them.

### **3. Is the auto-numbering feature available to all users?**
No. The auto-numbering feature is currently in beta and can be enabled in Bluesky’s settings. It is not yet available to all users.

### **4. Will Bluesky introduce more privacy controls in the future?**
While Bluesky has not announced specific plans, the platform’s focus on user autonomy suggests that more privacy controls are likely in the pipeline.
