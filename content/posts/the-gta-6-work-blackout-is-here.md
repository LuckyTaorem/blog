## Potential Risks and Counterarguments

While many celebrate the corporate embrace of GTA VI, critics warn of unintended consequences:

- **Equity concerns** – Not all employees have the same access to gaming hardware or the desire to play, potentially creating a divide between “gamers” and “non‑gamers.”
- **Productivity spillover** – Some managers fear that the day off could set a precedent, leading to future requests for other high‑profile releases (e.g., *The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom* sequel).
- **Legal considerations** – In jurisdictions with strict labor laws, granting a “paid day off for gaming” could be interpreted as a benefit that must be uniformly offered, raising compliance costs.

Companies are navigating these challenges by framing the day as a **flexible floating holiday** rather than a mandatory paid leave, allowing employees to schedule it at a convenient time within the year.

**Q: Are there any tax implications for the Play Fund stipend?**
