## Why the BOGO Ticket Offer Matters for Founders

The “Buy One, Get One Free” (BOGO) promotion for the TechCrunch Founder Summit isn’t just a clever marketing gimmick; it’s a strategic lever that can reshape how early‑stage teams allocate resources. For a typical startup, travel, lodging, and conference fees quickly eat into a modest runway. By effectively halving the cost of admission, the deal frees up capital that can be redirected toward product development, hiring, or a crucial seed round.

Beyond the immediate financial relief, the BOGO structure encourages **co‑founder participation**. Many founders attend events solo, missing out on the synergy that arises when both visionaries experience the same sessions, network together, and leave with a shared narrative. Bringing a partner or a key team member amplifies the learning curve and multiplies the number of connections made—an exponential benefit that aligns with the “network effect” principle that underpins successful startups.

The timing of the promotion is also deliberate. Doors open at 9 a.m. ET on November 4, and the price increase is scheduled for later in the day.
