## Technical Breakdown: How the Snap Grip Stand Works

### Magnetic Architecture

At the heart of the Snap Grip Stand is a **double‑sided magnet** that secures the phone to the grip while allowing the device to pivot freely. The magnets are embedded in a thin, flexible polymer layer that conforms to the phone’s back surface. This design ensures:

- **Consistent pull force** across a range of phone models, from iPhone 13 to Samsung Galaxy S23.
- **Low magnetic field exposure** to internal components, mitigating interference with sensitive electronics.
- **Easy detachment** when the user needs to remove the phone, achieved by a slight lateral twist that disengages the magnetic coupling.

### Material Selection

The stand’s body is crafted from a high‑strength polycarbonate blend, providing durability while keeping the weight under 30 g. The black and silver color options not only match most phone cases but also offer a subtle contrast that enhances visibility on bright screens.

### Ergonomic Considerations

The grip’s curvature aligns with the natural hand contour, reducing strain during extended use. The stand’s vertical angle is adjustable between 90° and 120°, allowing users to find the most comfortable viewing position. This flexibility is crucial for accessibility, ensuring that people with different hand sizes or visual preferences can benefit from the accessory.
