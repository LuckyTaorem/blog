## Technical Breakdown

### Charging Architecture

- **iPhone (MagSafe):** Up to 15 W via MFi‑certified Qi coil. The coil aligns automatically thanks to MagSafe magnets, ensuring optimal power transfer even when the pole is extended.
- **Apple Watch:** Dedicated 5 W inductive pad on the side of the pole. The watch sits upright, making it easy to glance at the time while it charges.
- **AirPods/Other Earbuds:** A weighted base houses a secondary Qi coil delivering 5 W. The base’s mass stabilizes the stand, preventing wobble when the pole is fully extended.

### Power Management

The included 36 W USB‑C adapter distributes power intelligently: it prioritizes the iPhone’s 15 W demand, then allocates remaining wattage to the Watch and earbuds. This dynamic allocation prevents overloading the power brick and maintains safe temperature thresholds.
