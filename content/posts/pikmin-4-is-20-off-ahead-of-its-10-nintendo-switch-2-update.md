### Switch 2 Edition: Technical Enhancements Over the Original

Nintendo’s upcoming **Switch 2 Edition** of *Pikmin 4* is slated for **November 12, 2026** and arrives with a suite of upgrades designed to leverage the next‑gen hardware. While the core gameplay loop—cultivating and commanding tiny plant‑like creatures—remains intact, several quality‑of‑life and visual improvements set this version apart:

- **Polished Visuals** – The Switch 2’s upgraded GPU renders higher‑resolution textures, more dynamic lighting, and smoother particle effects, giving the lush world of Pikinia a richer, more immersive look.
- **Voice‑Command Integration** – Players can now issue simple voice cues to the canine companion, Moe, to direct Pikmin squads, issue “hold” or “attack” orders, and even trigger special abilities without pausing the action.
- **Dandori Academy Mode** – A brand‑new challenge system that unlocks weekly puzzles, time‑trial missions, and leaderboards. Completing these tasks grants exclusive cosmetic skins and a small amount of in‑game currency.
- **Expanded Multiplayer** – Up to four players can now cooperate online in a “Co‑Op Expedition” mode, sharing resources and tackling larger boss encounters that were previously exclusive to local play.
- **Performance Optimizations** – Faster load times, reduced frame‑rate drops in dense Pikmin swarms, and an optional “Performance Mode” that locks the game at 60 fps on the Switch 2.
