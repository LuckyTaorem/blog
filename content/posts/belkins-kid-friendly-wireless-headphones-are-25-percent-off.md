## Sonos Ace Lineup – Value, Evolution, and Future Outlook

### Original Sonos Ace – $299.99

The original Ace model has been praised for its soundstage and seamless integration with the Sonos ecosystem. At $299.99 on Amazon and Best Buy, it undercuts competing true‑wireless earbuds that often start at $350. However, early 2024 reviews flagged two pain points:

1. **“Iffy” app experience** – occasional Bluetooth handoff glitches and a clunky UI for EQ adjustments.  
2. **Lack of TV Audio Swap support** – the headphones could not pair with Sonos Beam or Ray soundbars for instant TV audio routing.

Sonos responded with a roadmap that included a full app overhaul and the addition of TV Audio Swap. The updated firmware, rolled out in Q3 2024, now lets users switch between music and TV audio with a single tap, effectively closing the feature gap with competitors like Bose and Sony.

### Sonos Ace Ultra – $449

The Ultra version builds on the original with three headline upgrades:

- **Extended battery life** – up to 30 hours of playback versus 20 hours on the base model.  
- **Enhanced active noise cancellation (ANC)** – dual‑mic feed‑forward algorithm reduces ambient noise by an additional 5 dB.  
- **Wi‑Fi linking to Sonos speakers** – a novel feature that allows the headphones to maintain a high‑bandwidth connection to home speakers, enabling “room‑free” listening without staying within Bluetooth range.

This Wi‑Fi link is a strategic move: it positions Sonos as a hybrid audio solution, blurring the line between traditional headphones and multi‑room speaker systems. For power users who stream high‑resolution audio (24‑bit/96 kHz), the Wi‑Fi path eliminates Bluetooth’s codec limitations, delivering lossless sound throughout the house.

### Future Outlook

Analysts predict that Sonos will continue to integrate its headphone line with its broader smart‑home roadmap. Expect future firmware updates to add:

- **Spatial audio processing** leveraging the existing Sonos Arc hardware.

**Q: Are Amazon Resale items covered by a warranty?**
